Copenhagen – Day 1 (Vesterbro & Nørrebro)

Crowne Plaza Copenhagen Towers base · Thu 19 Mar · Chill, cafes, bars, young/hipster areas

Itinerary

  • 12:30 – leave hotel
    Crowne Plaza Copenhagen Towers (Ørestad)
    Walk to Ørestad station and head towards the city.
  • 13:00 – lunch
    Mad & Kaffe (Vesterbro)
    Brunch-style plates, young local crowd on Sønder Boulevard.
  • 14:15 – 15:00 – walk & explore
    Sønder Boulevard & Vesterbro streets
    Slow walk up and down Sønder Boulevard, drift towards Meatpacking (Kødbyen).
  • 15:00 – coffee
    Prolog Coffee Bar (Kødbyen)
    Third-wave coffee in the Meatpacking District.
  • 15:45 – 17:30 – Nørrebro wandering
    Jægersborggade & Nørrebro
    Indie shops, natural wine, cafes. Young, local, hipster vibe. Walk via the Lakes / Dronning Louises Bro if you feel like it.
  • 18:30 – wine
    Pompette (natural wine, Nørrebro)
    Natural wine bar, relaxed and busy. Good place to settle in before cocktails.
  • 20:00 – cocktails
    The Barking Dog (cocktail pub)
    Pub-feel cocktail bar. Easy, unpretentious, young crowd.
  • ~22:00 – head back
    Ørestad Station → Crowne Plaza
    Metro M1 back to Ørestad, short walk to the hotel. Crash.

Tip: on your phone, tap the place name to open it in Google Maps, then "Save" it into a list so you can see everything on one map.
