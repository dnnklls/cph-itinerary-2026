✦ Free landmarks (centre block)

Nyhavn
17th‑century waterfront with colourful houses and boats. Very touristy but still pretty; easy to combine with canal tour.
The Little Mermaid
Small bronze statue on the waterfront. Iconic, not huge – treat it as a bonus on a harbour walk, not the main event.
Amalienborg Palace
Royal square with four identical palaces. Changing of the guard at noon; easy to swing past between Nyhavn and the harbour.
Strøget
Pedestrian shopping street running across the old town. Free to walk, full of shops and street performers.
Christiansborg Palace Tower
Free elevator to a panoramic city view. Usually open late; queues can be long but the view is worth it.

☕ More cafes like Mad & Kaffe

Cadence Vesterbro
Chef‑y brunch plates in a leafy, light space. Very similar build‑your‑own vibe to Mad & Kaffe.
Sixteen Twelve Nørrebro
Brunch with twists on classics on Jægersborggade. Same young/hipster energy, different dishes.
Sidecar Nørrebro
Cosy, hygge brunch spot with brunch boards and a busy, young crowd.
Wulff & Konstali Various
Instagram‑famous brunch boards where you pick several small dishes – very close in spirit to Mad & Kaffe.

★ Hidden / speakeasy‑ish bars

Curfew
Speakeasy‑style cocktail bar with a library entrance and 1920s/30s vibe. Ring the buzzer; book if you care about seats.
Lidkoeb
Hidden in a backyard in Vesterbro with a top‑floor whisky lounge – feels like a townhouse you stumble into.
The Union Local
Small, dimly lit sister bar to The Union Kitchen, just off Nyhavn. Proper speakeasy feel and strong cocktails.
1656 Cocktail Bar
Graffiti‑covered exterior, cosy interior, seasonal cocktail list. Feels like you’ve found a secret.
Pulp Bar
Playful, retro bar in Nørrebro with colourful drinks and a young local crowd.
Bar Vitrine
Intimate natural‑wine bar and small‑plates restaurant in the centre. Newer opening with serious food and relaxed energy.
Café Gazou
French‑leaning neighbourhood café in Nordvest. Simple seasonal dishes, very local vibe.
Graziano
Tuscan trattoria in Nørrebro – comforting pastas and stews, generous portions, buzzy room.
Texture
Moody basement fine‑dining spot with a Michelin star. French‑leaning tasting menu, very polished.
Koan
Two‑star Danish‑Korean tasting menu in Carlsberg Byen. High‑concept, special‑occasion meal.
Reffen
Huge outdoor street‑food market on Refshaleøen – global stalls, harbour views, music. Go when it’s not freezing.
TorvehallerneKBH
Glass‑covered food halls by Nørreport – smørrebrød, tacos, coffee, and good grazing.
Juno the Bakery
Cult bakery in Østerbro. Cardamom buns and croissants worth a small queue.
Hart Bageri
Ex‑Noma bakery with multiple branches. Sourdough and laminated pastries done properly.
Lille Bakery
Bakery–café in an old industrial hall on Refshaleøen. Great bread and low‑key brunch plates.
SMK – National Gallery of Denmark
Big airy art museum with Danish and European collections. Solid rainy‑day anchor.
Ny Carlsberg Glyptotek
Palm‑filled winter garden plus sculpture and Impressionists near Tivoli. Very atmospheric.
Designmuseum Danmark
Danish design museum – chairs, lamps, everyday objects. If you care about interiors, go.
Danish Architecture Center (DAC) & BLOX
Architecture and city‑making exhibitions in a bold waterfront building. Nice harbour views and café.
Operaparken
New park next to the opera with layered landscaping and views back to the city.
Superkilen Park
Colourful, design‑heavy park in Nørrebro showing Copenhagen's multicultural side.
Frederiksberg Gardens
Large romantic park with canals and views into the zoo. Good on a sunny slow day.
CopenHill (Amager Bakke)
Ski slope / hiking trail on top of a waste‑to‑energy plant. Outrageously "future city" and very fun photos.
Nordhavn & Konditaget Lüders
New harbour district plus rooftop playground with skyline views. Catnip for architecture / urbanism people.
Louisiana Museum of Modern Art (day trip)
Modern‑art museum and sculpture park right on the sea. Easy train up the coast; strong candidate for a half‑ or full‑day.
CopenHot
Wood‑fired hot tubs and saunas by the harbour on Refshaleøen. Alternate hot/cold with city views – especially good when it’s chilly.
Sauna House
Design‑y sauna + harbour‑dip spot in Nordhavn with guided saunagus sessions. Book slots.
Islands Brygge Harbour Baths
Local swim zone and lawn for hanging out with beers. Peak Copenhagen on a warm evening.
Ruby
Townhouse cocktail bar in the city centre. Polished but not stiff – good for date‑night energy.
Jolene & Kødbyen bars
Meatpacking District cluster of bars and casual clubs. Easy late‑night finish after dinner there or nearby.
VEGA / Loppen / Byhaven
VEGA for bigger acts, Loppen in Christiania for gritty alt shows, Byhaven (summer) for free outdoor gigs at Pumpehuset.
Guided bike tours
Easy overview of several neighbourhoods in a few hours without planning your own route. Good early‑trip activity.
Copenhagen food tours
Small‑group food walks (smørrebrød, pastries, cheese, beer). Combine with centre sightseeing.
