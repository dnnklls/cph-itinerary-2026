Copenhagen – Day 1 (Vesterbro → City → Nørrebro loop)

Crowne Plaza Copenhagen Towers base · Thu 19 Mar · Cafes, bars, young/hipster areas · One clean loop (no backtracking)

Main route

  • 12:30 – leave hotel
    Crowne Plaza Copenhagen Towers (Ørestad)
    Base. Walk to Ørestad Station and head into the city on Metro M1.
  • ~13:00 – lunch
    Mad & Kaffe (Vesterbro)
    Brunch-style plates on Sønder Boulevard. Young local crowd, good first stop after landing.
    View photos on Google Maps
  • 14:00 – Vesterbro walk + coffee
    Sønder Boulevard → Meatpacking (Kødbyen) → Prolog Coffee Bar
    Slow walk along Sønder Boulevard, then drift into Kødbyen. Grab third‑wave coffee at Prolog in the Meatpacking District.
    View Prolog on Maps
  • 15:30 – 17:30 – City centre + Nyhavn cluster
    Strøget, Christiansborg Tower, Nyhavn, Amalienborg, Little Mermaid (optional)
    From Vesterbro, head via København H into the old town. Walk Strøget, take the free view from Christiansborg Tower if you want, then Nyhavn and Amalienborg. If you still have legs, do the Little Mermaid loop and come back via the harbour.
  • ~17:30 – 19:00 – Nørrebro walk
    Lakes / Dronning Louises Bro → Jægersborggade
    Metro or walk up to Nørrebro. Cross the Lakes via Dronning Louises Bro, then wander Jægersborggade for shops and cafes.
  • 19:00 – wine
    Pompette (natural wine, Nørrebro)
    Natural wine bar, relaxed and busy. Good place to settle in before cocktails.
    View photos on Google Maps
  • 20:30 – cocktails
    The Barking Dog (cocktail pub, Nørrebro)
    Pub‑feel cocktail bar. Easy, unpretentious, lots of young people. Stay here or hop to Pulp Bar if you want something more playful.
    View photos on Google Maps
  • ~22:00 – back to hotel
    Nørrebro → Ørestad Station → Crowne Plaza
    From Nørrebro St. or Nørreport, metro back to Ørestad (M3/M4 → M1). Short walk from Ørestad station to the hotel.

Optional free landmarks (anytime in the centre block)

  • Nyhavn (colourful harbour)
    17th-century waterfront with brightly coloured townhouses. Free to walk, very touristy but pretty.
  • The Little Mermaid statue
    Small bronze statue on the waterfront. Touristy but iconic. About 15 min walk from Nyhavn.
  • Amalienborg Palace (changing of guard)
    Royal palace with four identical buildings. Changing of guard at 12:00 daily. Free to watch from the square.
  • Strøget (pedestrian shopping street)
    One of Europe's longest pedestrian streets. Free to walk, lots of shops and street performers.
  • Christiansborg Palace Tower (free view)
    Free elevator to the tower for panoramic city views. Usually open until 21:00. Queue can be long but worth it.

More cafes like Mad & Kaffe

  • Cadence (Vesterbro)
    Creative, chef-y brunch plates in a leafy, light space. Very similar energy to Mad & Kaffe.
  • Sixteen Twelve (Nørrebro, Jægersborggade)
    “Next-level” brunch with twists on classics, cool interiors, same kind of crowd as Mad & Kaffe.
  • Sidecar (Nørrebro)
    Cosy, hygge brunch spot with brunch boards and a busy, young vibe. Good backup if Mad & Kaffe is slammed.
  • Wulff & Konstali
    Instagram brunch boards (pick small dishes), very similar build-your-own style to Mad & Kaffe.

Hidden / speakeasy-ish bars

  • Curfew (Vesterbro)
    Speakeasy-style cocktail bar with library entrance and 1920s/30s vibe; buzzer and reservations recommended.
  • Lidkoeb (Vesterbro)
    Backyard entrance and top-floor whisky lounge give it hidden-bar energy.
  • The Union Local (near Nyhavn)
    Small, dimly lit, speakeasy-feel sister bar to The Union Kitchen; very cocktail-focused.
  • 1656 Cocktail Bar (Vesterbro)
    Graffiti-covered exterior, cosy interior, strong seasonal cocktail list.
  • Pulp Bar (Nørrebro)
    Playful, retro speakeasy-ish bar with colourful drinks and a young local crowd.
